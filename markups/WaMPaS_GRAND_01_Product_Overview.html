01 — Executive Summary

WaMPaS is a unified, three-layer Christian platform for Nigeria and the African diaspora. It serves as a permanent sermon archive, a living events registry, and a moral-oriented social network — built as one application whose depth expands over time.

Mission

To become the spiritual home of African Christianity on the internet — where every teaching is preserved, every event is remembered, and every believer belongs.

02 — The Three Layers
Layer 1
The Archive
A permanent, searchable record of what the Church has taught
  • Verbatim messages, quotes, and sermon summaries posted by congregants
  • Indexed by pastor, church, denomination, topic, category, date, and event
  • Full pastor and church profiles — community-maintained, trust-tiered
  • Bible reference tagging and cross-referencing across the entire archive
  • Immutable data design — verified core records cannot be silently altered
  • Denomination HQ access to monitor branch teachings (Phase 3)
Layer 2
The Events Layer
A living record of the Church's gatherings — past, present, and future
  • Concluded events: messages preached, pastors present, testimonies
  • Upcoming events: promotional listings for crusades, conferences, revivals
  • Sponsored event promotion: churches pay to surface events to the audience
  • Calendar integration: believers save upcoming events to Google Calendar
  • Testimony wall: miracle and testimony accounts per event
Layer 3
The Social Platform
A moral-oriented, values-governed Christian social network
  • Public feed: posts, reflections, scripture sharing, quote sharing
  • Fellowship rooms: persistent church-affiliated community group spaces
  • Prayer rooms: live and asynchronous prayer sessions with answered-prayer archive
  • Direct fellowship: one-to-one messaging governed by community standards
  • Moral governance: community code of conduct enforced through peer + elder review
  • Verified pastor presence: pastors post, answer questions, lead prayer rooms
03 — Why It Will Work

No direct competitor exists. WhatsApp is ephemeral. Facebook is ungoverned. Sermon Audio is not African. WaMPaS is purpose-built for this community — from the cultural language of the UI to the payment methods to the governance framework.

A living archive and social home for African Christianity — where every sermon is preserved, every event is remembered, and every believer belongs.
