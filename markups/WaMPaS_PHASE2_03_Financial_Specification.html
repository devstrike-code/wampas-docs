01 — Phase 2 Financial Position

Phase 2 introduces the first revenue streams. The platform begins earning from church event advertising and user donations. Revenue should cover operating costs by month 8, making the platform cash-flow positive before Phase 3 investment begins.

Phase 2 break-even is at roughly 10 active event ads per month — a very achievable number with 500+ church profiles on the platform. The platform should be cash-flow positive by month 7–8.
