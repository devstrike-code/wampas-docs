05 — HQ Licensing Sales Strategy

HQ denomination deals have long sales cycles (6–12 months from first contact to signed agreement). Begin outreach in Phase 2 (Month 8) by identifying the tech or communications lead at RCCG, Winners Chapel, COZA, Deeper Life, and the Catholic Bishops Conference of Nigeria.

  • Month 8: Informal outreach — share platform demo, ask for feedback
  • Month 10: Formal proposal — tailored to each denomination's governance concerns
  • Month 12: Pilot offer — 3-month free pilot with full HQ dashboard access
  • Month 14–15: Conversion to paid annual licence after pilot
