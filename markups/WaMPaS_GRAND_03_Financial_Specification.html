01 — Financial Philosophy

WaMPaS is built on a trust-first monetisation model. No core spiritual action is ever paywalled. Revenue comes from institutions, promotional placement, and premium users who want more — never from users who simply want to read and share God's word.

Core Principle

Earn the right to charge by delivering irreplaceable value first. Each phase of monetisation is gated behind phase-specific content and user density thresholds.
