01 — Phase 1 Objective

Phase 1 answers one question: will Nigerian Christians post their pastor's messages on a public, structured platform? Everything built in Phase 1 is in service of seeding the archive with credible, attributed content and proving the core sharing loop.

Phase 1 Definition of Success

1,000 messages posted by 500 unique posters, spanning 5+ denominations, with a measurable WhatsApp referral traffic pattern confirming the share-card loop is working.

02 — What Is Built
Layer 1
The Archive
A permanent, searchable record of what the Church has taught
  • Verbatim messages, quotes, and sermon summaries posted by congregants
  • Indexed by pastor, church, denomination, topic, category, date, and event
  • Full pastor and church profiles — community-maintained, trust-tiered
  • Bible reference tagging and cross-referencing across the entire archive
  • Immutable data design — verified core records cannot be silently altered
  • Denomination HQ access to monitor branch teachings (Phase 3)

Events (Core)

  • Concluded event pages
  • Event-linked messages
  • Testimony submission
  • Basic upcoming event listings (free, no payment)
03 — What Is Deliberately NOT Built
  • Social feed, fellowship rooms, prayer rooms — not yet
  • Paid ad subscriptions — content density needed first
  • Verified profiles — trust must be earned before it is sold
  • Push notifications / PWA — Phase 2 retention mechanic
  • AI search — Phase 3 after archive has sufficient depth
Why This Restraint Matters

Launching with everything signals a platform that doesn't know what it is. Phase 1 is a quote archive. That clarity is what makes the first 500 users trust it.

05 — Go-to-Market

Church Anchor Strategy

Identify one influential pastor per major denomination in Lagos and Abuja. Visit their church office. Demonstrate the quote card in person. Ask them to post their next Sunday message. Their congregation follows.

WhatsApp Flywheel

Every message generates a shareable 1080×1080 image card with the quote, pastor name, church, and WaMPaS logo. This card is designed specifically for WhatsApp status. When someone sees it and asks 'where is this from?' — the link points back to WaMPaS.
