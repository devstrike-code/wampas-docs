01 — Phase 3 Objective

Phase 3 completes the platform vision. The Social layer goes fully live with fellowship rooms, prayer rooms, and direct messaging. Denomination HQ licensing begins. The platform transitions from a content destination to a daily spiritual home.

Layer 3
The Social Platform
A moral-oriented, values-governed Christian social network
  • Public feed: posts, reflections, scripture sharing, quote sharing
  • Fellowship rooms: persistent church-affiliated community group spaces
  • Prayer rooms: live and asynchronous prayer sessions with answered-prayer archive
  • Direct fellowship: one-to-one messaging governed by community standards
  • Moral governance: community code of conduct enforced through peer + elder review
  • Verified pastor presence: pastors post, answer questions, lead prayer rooms
02 — New Capabilities Unlocked

Fellowship Rooms

  • Persistent group spaces: church cell groups, Bible study groups, topic-based rooms, denomination-wide rooms
  • Owner + up to 3 elders per room. Elders can warn, mute, remove members.
  • Full message history — new members can read past discussions
  • Linked to church profiles — surfaced on the church's public page
  • Cross-denomination rooms with enhanced moderation layer

Prayer Rooms

  • Async mode: open prayer request threads, any member can intercede
  • Live mode: timed (30–90 min) pastor-led prayer sessions with structured turns
  • Anonymous prayer requests — name hidden but still account-tied for moderation
  • Stand in agreement: intercessor count visible per request
  • Answered prayer archive: poster marks request answered, writes testimony
  • Verified pastor presence highlighted in any room they enter

Platform Tier

  • Denomination HQ licensing dashboard — monitor all branch messages and event activity
  • White-label option for large denominations
  • AI-powered semantic search across the full archive
  • Multi-language UI: Yoruba, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin, French (West Africa expansion)
  • Premium subscription tier — ad-free, curated message booklets, early features
03 — Moral Governance Framework

The social layer is governed by a moral framework derived from Christian values, enforced through platform mechanics — not just community guidelines.

PrinciplePlatform Mechanism
TruthAll content is attributed. No anonymous public posting. Misattribution is a reportable offence.
EdificationPosts must build up the body. Content that tears down or creates division is reportable within 24 hours.
AccountabilityElder roles within fellowship rooms mirror church governance. Three warnings escalate to platform moderation.
Restoration not punishmentBanned users receive a restoration path — warning → 7-day suspension → 30-day + elder review.
Denominational respectNo content may mock or attack another denomination. Cross-denominational rooms have extra moderation.
Sexual purityExplicit content results in immediate permanent ban. Non-negotiable. Stated clearly in onboarding.
