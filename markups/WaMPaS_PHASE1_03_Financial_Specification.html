01 — Phase 1 Financial Position

Phase 1 is a deliberate investment phase. Zero revenue is generated. The goal is content seeding and habit formation. Introducing monetisation before critical mass dilutes trust and slows adoption.

Rule

Do not introduce any paid feature until Phase 1 KPIs are met: 1,000 messages, 500 users, confirmed WhatsApp referral loop.
